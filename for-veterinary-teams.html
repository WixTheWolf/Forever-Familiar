FOR VETERINARY, HOSPICE & AFTERCARE TEAMS

A gentler resource to offer after goodbye.

Families often leave with the medical details handled and the emotional details suddenly everywhere. Forever Familiar gives them a private, at-home way to preserve the habits, routines, and memories they are afraid of forgetting.

WHY IT FITS AFTERCARE

It does not try to replace grief support.

Private

The current studio processes the family's pet photo and memories in their browser.

Self-paced

Families can create the tribute at home when they are ready.

Specific

The prompts focus on the pet's real habits and personality rather than generic memorial language.

Forever Familiar is a creative memorial product, not veterinary care, grief counseling, or mental-health treatment.
