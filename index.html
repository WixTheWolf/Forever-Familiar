QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Do I have to pay before I enter my pet’s information?

No. Add the photo and memories first. Your draft is saved on this device. You pay $29 only when you choose to unlock the private studio and six finished keepsakes.

What do I receive?

Memorial letter, 8×10 keepsake, phone wallpaper, social tribute, remembrance reading, and Little Things memory piece.

Is this only for dogs and cats?

No. The example library includes a horse and rabbit specifically because companion loss isn't limited to dogs and cats.

Do I need a perfect photo?

No. Use the photograph that feels most like them. Ordinary photos are often the most meaningful.

Is the example content from real customers?

No. The current examples are clearly marked fictional profiles. We won't present fictional stories as customer testimonials.
