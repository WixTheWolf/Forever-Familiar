# Forever Familiar — 30-Day Organic Launch Plan

## Launch rule
Do not spend meaningful ad money until Stripe review is complete, the domain is connected, the after-payment redirect works, and one end-to-end purchase has been tested.

## Week 1 — Trust + search foundation
- Publish homepage with Charlie input→output and six-piece product spread.
- Publish guides: dog-loss words, cat-loss words, pet memorial ideas, free message maker.
- Create Pinterest business profile after domain is live.
- Publish 2 pins/day from the six launch designs, rotating title/description.
- Create Google Search Console property and submit sitemap after domain is live.
- Contact 5 independent in-home euthanasia / aftercare providers with a no-pressure partner pilot note.

## Week 2 — Example storytelling
- Publish one Charlie Reel and one Daisy Reel.
- Post Luna carousel: "The quiet things you miss about a cat."
- Post Daisy carousel: "A memorial doesn't have to make your dog sound like a Victorian poet."
- Add Jasper and Mochi examples to landing page.
- Contact 10 additional local/regional pet-care partners.

## Week 3 — Conversion learning
- Review Stripe checkout starts vs. completed purchases.
- Review which guides/pins drive visits.
- Ask every legitimate buyer for short feedback 3 days after purchase.
- Rewrite the weakest homepage section based on actual questions/support requests.
- If there are 3+ sales, test one $25 Pinterest traffic campaign to the best-performing guide—not directly to checkout.

## Week 4 — Double down, don't decorate
- If one search topic gets traction, publish 3 supporting articles around it.
- If partner replies outperform social, spend time on partner outreach instead of more Instagram posts.
- If visitors reach checkout but don't buy, improve product proof and checkout—not traffic volume.
- If 10 paid orders are reached with low support burden, build referral code workflow and automated review request.

## First validation gate
- 200 qualified visits
- 10 paid orders
- refund rate < 8%
- at least 3 unsolicited positive customer comments
- at least 1 organic or partner-referred sale

Only after this gate consider recurring paid traffic.
