SYMPATHY CARD MESSAGES

Pet Loss Sympathy Card Messages That Do Not Sound Generic

Short is fine. The goal is to acknowledge the relationship, not write a perfect speech.

If you knew the pet

“I’m so sorry about Max. I’ll always remember how he brought you a toy the second anyone walked through the door. He was such a big part of your home.”

If you did not know the pet

“I’m so sorry about Luna. I know how much she meant to you and how much of your daily life she was part of. I’m thinking about you.”

For a longtime companion

“Fourteen years is a whole chapter of life to share with someone. I’m so sorry. I hope the familiar little memories start to feel comforting in time.”

For a small pet

“Mochi may have been small, but I know the space she held in your life was not. I’m thinking about you.”

What to leave out

Avoid “at least,” replacement-pet suggestions, timelines for grief, or spiritual claims unless you know the person shares them.
